# Profile and badges

A ticket holder who has entered an event opens their ticket and presses **Crear
mi perfil** under **Tus insignias**. The panel then shows a **Ver mi perfil**
button to the profile's link:

```
https://up.rising.sh/p/u1GJWEy0o_9zPnKvymZK9g
```

The link opens a page with the holder's first name and one badge for each
published event they entered, newest first. Each badge takes its event's colors
and links to the event. A ticket counts once an admin registers its entry at the
door; see [Check in at the door](check-in.md).

The profile belongs to the person, not the ticket: every ticket under the same
email shows the same link, and a later check-in adds a badge to it. Search
engines do not index it.

**Ocultar mi perfil** turns the link off, and the old link answers 404. Pressing
**Crear mi perfil** again makes a new link.

## Roles

An admin gives people a role at an event on its **Equipo** tab; see
[Team](add-an-event.md#team). The roles are Organiza, Ponente, Mentor, Jurado
and Voluntario. The event page lists the people under one card per role.

A role belongs to the person's account, so the event joins their profile and
**Tus roles** on their account page. Pages never show the email.

- Their ticket offers **Crear mi perfil** before any check-in.
- Their profile lists the published events of each role under its own heading,
  above the badges.
- Once their profile is open, the event page links their name to it.

A role holder opens a profile from their account page, with or without a ticket.
